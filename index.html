Meet the Lead Dentist

Dr. Claire Sterling, DDS, FAGD

Founder & Clinical Director · Cosmetic & Implant Specialist

Dr. Sterling founded Apex Dental Studio with a singular philosophy: exceptional dentistry should never feel rushed, intimidating, or impersonal. Combining biomimetic restorative techniques with an artist's eye for natural facial harmony, she creates results that are both enduring and effortlessly understated.

Every new patient consultation begins with an unhurried conversation—not a lecture—so you understand your options clearly and feel genuinely cared for at every step.
